FAQ

What is an eSIM?
An eSIM is a digital SIM that lets you activate a mobile data plan without using a physical SIM card. It’s built into supported devices and can be enabled in your phone settings.
How do I activate an eSIM?
Activation typically happens by scanning a QR code or installing an eSIM profile from an app/portal, then turning on the plan in your device’s cellular settings.
Do I need to remove my physical SIM?
In most cases, no. You can keep your physical SIM active and use the eSIM for data while traveling, depending on your device and plan settings.
Will my eSIM work immediately when I land?
Once the eSIM profile is installed and the plan is enabled, your device can connect to supported networks in the destination — usually within a few moments.
Can I use the same eSIM on multiple devices?
Most eSIM profiles are intended for one device at a time. If you change devices, you may need to reinstall the profile or purchase a new plan, depending on the provider’s rules.
